About Leye Auto Group LLC

A company connecting customers in the United States and Nigeria to vehicles and automotive spare parts.

Who We Are

Leye Auto Group LLC was founded by Opaleye Mofolaji Opeyemi in octomber 2016. The company sources and purchases vehicles in the United States, sources new and used automotive spare parts, and arranges shipping of purchased vehicles from the USA to Africa.

We work directly with customers to understand what they need whether that is a specific vehicle, a particular spare part, or help getting a purchased vehicle shipped to Africa and confirm the relevant details and costs for each request individually.

We do not publish invented company history, years of experience, or customer numbers. Verified details will be added here once confirmed by the founder.

Opaleye Mofolaji Opeyemi

What We Do

Vehicle Sourcing

Finding and helping customers purchase vehicles located in the United States.

Spare-Parts Sourcing

Sourcing new and used automotive spare parts based on vehicle and part details provided by the customer.

Shipping Arrangements

Arranging the shipment of purchased vehicles from the USA to Africa.
