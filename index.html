About Leye Auto Group LLC

Leye Auto Group LLC, founded by Opaleye Mofolaji Opeyemi, works with customers in the United States and Nigeria to source vehicles, locate new and used automotive spare parts, and arrange shipping of purchased vehicles from the USA to Africa. Every request is handled individually, with details and costs confirmed directly with the customer.
