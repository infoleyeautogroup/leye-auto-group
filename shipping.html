USA to Nigeria Vehicle Shipping

Once a vehicle has been purchased, Leye Auto Group LLC arranges and coordinates its shipment from the United States to Nigeria.

Overview

Leye Auto Group LLC's confirmed role is to arrange the shipping of purchased vehicles from the United States to Nigeria. Shipping arrangements, estimated costs, documentation, and applicable charges are discussed and confirmed for each request individually.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does shipping cost? +

Shipping costs depend on the vehicle, route, and current market rates. We confirm estimated costs for each request individually — costs are not published on this website.

How long does shipping take? +

Transit times vary and have not been confirmed for publication. We will discuss expected timing with you as part of your specific shipping arrangement.

Do you handle customs clearance in Nigeria? +

This has not yet been confirmed. Please contact us directly to discuss what is included in your specific shipping arrangement.

Can you ship a vehicle I purchased elsewhere? +

Contact us with details of the vehicle and its current location, and we will advise on whether we can assist with arranging shipping.

Owner Review Checklist, Confirm Before Publishing

  • Confirm whether the company handles customs clearance in Nigeria or whether this is a third-party service.
  • Confirm whether insurance is offered or arranged during shipping.
  • Confirm typical shipping routes/ports used (if any can be disclosed).
  • Confirm whether delivery is port-to-port or includes inland transport in Nigeria.
  • Confirm any vehicle eligibility restrictions (age, type, condition).
  • Confirm whether estimated transit times can be shared publicly.
